Columbia, CA · Founded 1850 · "Gem of the Southern Mines"
COLUMBIA STATE HISTORIC PARK
30 original 1850s brick buildings still in active use. Explore the saloon, stagecoach rides, gold panning at Matelot Gulch, candle dipping, and antique bowling alley. No cars in town. 11am guided tour — arrive at 10am opening to explore first. Free admission. Open daily 10am–5pm.

Jamestown, CA · Founded 1848 · Gateway to the Mother Lode
RAILTOWN 1897 STATE HISTORIC PARK
Working steam locomotive roundhouse — these engines appeared in Back to the Future Part III, High Noon, and 200+ films. Arrive early to explore the roundhouse, then board the 3pm excursion train (45 min ride). Open daily 9:30am–4:30pm.

MURPHYS MAIN STREET
The most polished town in Gold Country. Art galleries, the historic Murphys Hotel bar (1856 — Ulysses S. Grant slept here), tasting rooms, boutiques. A relaxed afternoon before dinner. This is the anchor of the day.
